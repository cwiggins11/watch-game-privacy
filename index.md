# Privacy Policy

Effective Date: February 23, 2026

This privacy policy applies to all applications ("Apps") developed and published by Christopher Wiggins unless otherwise stated within a specific app listing.

## Information Collection and Use

Our Apps do **not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information**.

Specifically, our Apps:

- Do not require user accounts
- Do not collect analytics data
- Do not use advertising SDKs
- Do not access contacts, location, health data, photos, microphone, camera, or other personal information
- Do not communicate with external servers
- Do not track users across apps or websites

Any data generated while using the App (such as scores, progress, or settings) is stored locally on the device and is not transmitted to us or any third party.

## Children's Privacy

Our Apps are safe for children and do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

## Third-Party Services

Our Apps do not integrate with third-party services that collect personal data.

## Data Security

Since we do not collect or transmit personal data, there is no personal information stored on external systems.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

If future versions of our Apps introduce features that require data collection, this privacy policy will be updated accordingly and reflected in the App Store listing.
